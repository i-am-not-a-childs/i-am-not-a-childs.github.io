Project Description

This project is about making a game called Robattles. I want this to be a game where, people can learn about robotics while having a lot of fun. This game is going to be a 64 bit, in a BattleBots kind of setting, and I have already started designing some things. I'm also going to be doing some research on world models which will be shown in the Blog. I have a GitHub page for my game Here.


My Vision

My vision for this game, is that it will be a BattleBots style of game in 64 bit where you learn just a little bit about robotics. I'm thinking that your robot will be controlled by an AI using world models. your enemy's robot will be controlled by an AI slightly worse than yours. The game will be a rogue-like, where you will defeat enemies to get some sort of material to purchase upgrades and to unlock new weapons and building materials. You'll be able to buy upgrades that give you three options of weapons or building materials to choose from; you can only choose one. You will start as the reigning champion, but someone unknown will reveal themselves and challenge you with a bet: the loser gives away everything. The challenger is too strong and you lose. You wake up two years later (It was a dream) and you decide to make your way back up the ladder and regain your title as champion. Every time you lose against the champion, you restart everything, just like a classic rogue-like. You would be fighting in what is basically a BattleBots arena, but from a top-down view. There would also be traps in the arena, just like BattleBots.
